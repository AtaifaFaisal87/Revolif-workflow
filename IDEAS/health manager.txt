================ AI ASSISTANT RELATIONSHIP ================


AI Assistant has an association relationship with HealthManager.

AI Assistant does not store health data.

It only reads health records when the user requests help.



Working:


User

↓

HealthManager

↓

AI Assistant

↓

Symptom Analysis

↓

Suggestions / Recommendations



Example:


Input:


Symptoms:

Cough + Fever + Fatigue



AI Response:


Possible causes:

- Common cold
- Flu


Suggestions:

- Rest
- Stay hydrated
- Monitor symptoms

- Consult doctor if symptoms become severe



================ USER AND HEALTH MANAGER RELATIONSHIP ================


The User has a composition relationship with HealthManager because health records belong to a specific user.

Each User contains their own HealthManager object, which manages all personal health records.

When a User exists, their HealthManager exists with them.

Each HealthRecord is linked to that user's ID.

Health data remains private and cannot be accessed by other users or unauthorized admins.



================ HEALTH MANAGER AND STATISTICS RELATIONSHIP ================


Statistics has an association relationship with HealthManager.

Statistics does not store health data.

It only reads HealthManager data and generates reports.



Example:


Monthly Health Report:


Common Symptoms:

Headache: 4 times

Fever: 2 times


Health Trend:

Improving


Most Frequent Issue:

Sleep Problems

================ DESIGN REASONING ================


HealthRecord represents one health event entered by the user.

HealthManager manages all health records of one user.

AI Assistant analyzes symptoms and provides suggestions but does not modify health data.

Statistics analyzes health history and generates health reports.

Composition keeps each user's medical information private.
