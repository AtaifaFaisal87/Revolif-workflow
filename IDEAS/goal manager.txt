USER, GOAL MANAGER AND ACHIEVEMENT MANAGER

The User has a composition relationship with both GoalManager and AchievementManager because goals and achievements are personal data belonging exclusively to that user.

Each User owns their own GoalManager, which stores and manages all of their goals, and their own AchievementManager, which stores all badges they have earned.

When a User completes a goal, the GoalManager updates the goal's status and notifies the AchievementManager. The AchievementManager evaluates predefined achievement rules and unlocks any earned badges.

Statistics can generate reports based on the user's goals and achievements, while administrators cannot access or modify users' personal goals or achievements, ensuring privacy and security. Each user's data remains isolated and linked only to that specific user account.
